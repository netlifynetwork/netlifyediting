Frequently asked questions

How do I send large video files?

Use a file-sharing link such as Google Drive or Dropbox in your project brief. A secure direct upload can be added when a backend is configured.

Can I request changes?

Yes. Each package includes a stated number of revision rounds. Additional changes can be quoted separately.

How long does editing take?

Turnaround depends on footage length, complexity and current workload. Your delivery date should be confirmed before editing starts.

How do I pay?

Payment methods and terms should be confirmed directly with the studio before work begins.
